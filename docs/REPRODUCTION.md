# Reproduction

Run commands from the checkout after installation. The CLI is `python -m sca_reproduction` (also `sca-reproduce`). It generates the experimental inputs, runs SCA and the comparison procedures, and reports their results.

## Dependency sequence and panels

The individual commands below use `--workspace outputs/paper --config configs/paper.json` by default. `prepare-data` is an explicit network step; other study commands do not download data.

1. `run assignment`: regenerate 4,800 datasets over 16 population laws × three sample sizes × 100 replications. Recompute full-sample verified regions, restricted/complete searches and population oracles, then the original nested splits, pilot rankings, LTT tests and coupled first-failure decisions. Development replications 0–9 (480 datasets) determine the original global pilot fraction from `{1/5, 1/2}` at cap `3/2`, with exact ties choosing `1/5`. Evaluation replications 10–99 (4,320 datasets) follow this frozen choice. Evaluation stops if the development choice differs from the paper configuration.
2. `run release`: 100 independent calibration/outcome blocks for each of the balanced/moderate ordered K64 law, skewed/weak ordered K64 law, and fixed IHDP-derived K16 population. Calibration `n=4000K`, outcome prefixes `m/K={16,256}`, capacities `distinct ceil(K/16),ceil(K/8),ceil(K/4),ceil(K/2),K`. SCA minimizes the certificate at at most B groups. The equal-calibration-mass baseline uses whole states and deterministic contiguous cuts. Raw budget `G=2`, admissible aggregate budget at most 3. All declared groups, including zero treated counts, are exported. Aggregate-only inference is evaluated in a separate interpreter using the release artifact.
3. `run efficiency`: recompute confidence-construction and oracle-gap diagnostics on the 4,800 fresh assignment records and 300 fresh release blocks. It reuses the SCA regions and recomputes the allocation-control and fixed-group pooled bounds. `run recovery` supplies 300 independent nested count paths at the 12 original sample sizes (2,000 through 3,000,000), two paired laws, known/unknown mass modes and three certificate constructions. Recovery evaluates certificates for the full-support cell. Run both components before `report efficiency`. Recovery also has its own `report recovery` tables.
4. `run split`: 48 conditions × 90 evaluation datasets × six procedures × three caps = 77,760 records. Reuses the newly generated assignment/split data and recomputed A/B/F prerequisites. Computes all-proposal held-out certification, full-sample proposal-list certification, and complete pilot-order search. No new observations are drawn.
5. `run precision`: reuses the freshly generated release observations/regions, selecting by assignments only at caps `1,11/10,5/4,3/2,2`. Capacities are fixed at 8/32/4 for the three laws; the illustrative total-width target is `1/5`. Includes all conditions, admissions/abstentions and raw references. The five-stage signed width decomposition remains descriptive, not independent causal effects.
6. `run outcome`: three controlled laws (K16 balanced/moderate, K64 balanced/moderate, K64 skewed/weak) × 100 blocks, profiles constant/aligned/alternating and prefixes `m/K={4,16,64,256}`. Recomputes selection and inference and checks the resulting records before reporting. This includes raw stratification and stronger pooled-raw LP references.
7. `run computation`: generates the count-only inputs, runs four separate K64/K256 preflights, freezes resource limits, then executes 200 primary cases (two laws × K16/32/64/128/256 × 20), 30 separate capacity providers (first five replicates at K64/128/256) and 20 dependent repeated cohorts (first five at K64/256, nested within primary). Exactly one timed process and one native numerical thread run at a time. K64 counts are generated by the release sampler before timing begins.

Generate numerical tables and summaries after each completed study with `python -m sca_reproduction report STUDY`. `report all` requires all experimental panels. Optional main-paper plotting and table assembly are documented in [PAPER_RESULTS.md](PAPER_RESULTS.md); neither launches experiments. Output fields use the identifiers below and exact rational encodings.

## Procedure definitions

The proposed SCA procedure is full-sample, principal Coupled Clopper–Pearson, complete combined-family search. Both fixed-cap and fixed-capacity objectives use it. Oracle laws are used only after selection for truth diagnostics.

| Output identifier | Role / definition |
|---|---|
| `coupled`, combined family, unknown masses; efficiency `original`; split F | Principal SCA procedure in the corresponding experiment. |
| `independent`, `allocation_control`, `pooled_alternative` | Certificate-construction or allocation ablations. The pooled assignment construction allocates `4alpha/5` to the raw region and `alpha/5` to prespecified fixed-tree pooled summaries. It is distinct from the pooled **raw outcome reference**. |
| `fixed`, `contiguous` family | Search-family restrictions (ablations), with original tie rules. |
| split A / `split_ltt` | Split-LTT baseline with unadjusted tests and first-failure stopping. |
| split B | Held-out coupled certification, saved proposals, first failure. |
| split C | Held-out coupled certification, every saved proposal; fewest passing cells. |
| split D | Full-sample coupled certification, every saved pilot proposal. |
| split E | Full-sample coupled certification, complete combined family using the pilot order. |
| `equal_calibration_mass` | Equal-mass estimated-propensity grouping baseline, certified on the same region. |
| `z_global`, `z_cellwise` | Two transported outcome enclosures from the same SCA aggregate release: global or cell-specific completed distortion budgets. |
| `raw_stratified`, `raw_standalone`, `raw_pooled` | Outcome references retaining raw-state information; standalone raw uses its separate error allocation. |
| population oracle quantities | Privileged diagnostics within the stated realized family; never operational certification inputs. |

For C, the held-out common confidence event covers every proposal, so continuing after a failure does not need a proposal union bound. For D/E/F the full-sample common event also covers partitions proposed using a subset of those observations. This argument does not change A's LTT rule. The six procedures are prespecified comparisons, not an adaptively selected best-of-six output. The split fraction was selected for LTT; C is the all-proposal coupled procedure at the frozen split, not an optimally tuned split procedure.

Pilot-order contiguous proposals need not belong to the full-data-order family. E separates proposal-list restrictions from order changes. For complete compatible calculations, C cannot select more cells than B, and E cannot select more than D. There is no general ordering between the two confidence regions or the two estimated orders. The saved-proposal list includes infinite pilot scores; rankings are not certificates.

## Randomness and numerical conventions

`sca_reproduction/assignment.py` uses PCG64 `SeedSequence([20261008,300,K,mass_index,overlap_index,geometry_index,size_index,replication])`, followed by the original NumPy multinomial and binomial calls in order. The fixed second tree uses `[20261008,101,K]`, and the permuted-law geometry `[20261008,102,K]`. Exact nominal laws retain the first K−1 stored binary masses and use the exact final residual; propensities use their stored binary values. Assignment sampling uses the NumPy multinomial and binomial generators; the outcome and release samplers below use exact dyadic thresholds.

Nested pilot splits use `[20261012,901,population_index,size_index,replication]`, sequential multivariate hypergeometric allocations in state/action order, fractions `1/5` then `1/2`, and the complete original proposal generator. Pilot scoring uses ordinary floating CP limits for ranking only. Certification uses verified endpoints and exact rational comparisons.

Outcome inference component streams use master 20261014. Release/precision use 2026101502 and separate development master 202610150201. Controlled outcome/release draws use the exact 128-bit dyadic categorical/Bernoulli threshold sampler; IHDP uses reference-record resampling. Recovery uses 2026101603 and separate development 202610160301, nested sufficient-count increments, fair binomials and exact bounded-integer exceptional-state Bernoulli draws at 4/13. Computation uses 2026101701 except the documented K64 release bridge. Source modules record complete component keys in each generated protocol. Paired procedures/capacities/prefixes reuse a block and do not become additional replications.

Assignment and split calibration use alpha `1/20`; native known-mass and unknown-mass confidence allocations are different. Outcome/release/precision use `1/40` calibration plus `1/80` raw and `1/80` aggregate outcome errors. Verified roots use a 48-bit outward endpoint grid, 128 initial bits through 1,024 bits, at most 192 evaluations and 5 seconds per endpoint. Trial ceilings are study-specific: assignment/split 1,000,000; release/outcome/precision/computation 2,000,000; large recovery 4,000,000. LP endpoints use time limits and checkable dual/feasible fallbacks. Numerical timeouts can affect fallback frequency on another machine; unresolved work is retained explicitly.

The common assignment event covers candidate search and capacities simultaneously. Outcome statements retain their actual pointwise scope. Individual binary Monte Carlo rates use two-sided CP95 where specified; paired differences use their original paired mean procedures. The split grid uses equal weights over 48 conditions and variance `H^-2 sum_h s_h^2/90`; condition-level differences use paired Student95. Efficiency retains its separate original block/condition convention. One-sided exact zero-failure bounds remain explicitly one-sided. Admission-conditioned widths are descriptive, and unconditional admitted misses are retained.

## Restart, cost and completion

Use a dedicated output workspace per configuration. Completed records are reused, not replaced, and incompatible frozen settings are rejected by the adapters. Restart the identical command to continue. An explicit `run STUDY --limit N` is a bounded prefix, not a replacement paper configuration; `N` counts that runner's tasks/blocks. Finish with the unbounded command. A recorded computation timeout/error is a completed status record and is not silently redrawn. Do not run two owners of the same workspace or other CPU-heavy work during computational timing.

The computation stage sets primary limits to `max(600 seconds, 4 × largest preflight)` and capacity limits to `max(1800 seconds, 8 × largest preflight)`, with an 8 GiB provider checkpoint guard. At K256, the measured primary cases took about a minute, while capacity minimization took many minutes and several GiB. Full reproduction can require many CPU-hours and tens of GiB of storage. Start with smoke and inspect per-case costs on your machine. Split/efficiency runs reuse cached work; the scaling study measures fresh-process costs separately.

Full reproduction ends with the original panel denominators and explicit finite/infinite/rejected/unresolved/fallback counts. A partial above-cap rejection is not a completed upper bound. A safe fallback does not establish an optimum. The fixed-capacity certificate is nonincreasing in the **at-most** group budget; exactly-B optima/refinements need not be monotone.

## Validation

Validation covers focused tests, bounded end-to-end runs, and agreement of the table and figure outputs with the paper's saved results. Complete fresh regeneration of all experimental panels has not been performed for this release.

Use the pinned dependencies for numerical reproduction. Runtime, memory use and resource-dependent fallback behavior can vary across machines.
